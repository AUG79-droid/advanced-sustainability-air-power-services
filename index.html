Frame comparable alternatives, choose a meaningful functional unit and diagnose lifecycle hotspots before selecting a solution.

Sustainable Flight Operations and Navigation

Sustainable Maintenance, MRO and Material Support

04DATA

Digital Solutions and Data-Driven In-Service Sustainability

Upgrades, Life Extension, Retirement and Circularity

Treat retrofit as a lifecycle investment, compare the same service over the same horizon and preserve value through controlled circular routes.
